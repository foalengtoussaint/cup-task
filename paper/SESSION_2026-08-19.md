## Diagnosed, NOT applied

**BA `0 × inf` bug.** `project_torch` clamps `z` to 1e-3, so a behind-camera point becomes a ×1000
amplifier; the distortion polynomial takes it to ~1e22 px; `linalg.norm` squares it and overflows
float32 to `inf`; `inf × w(=0)` = NaN; strong-Wolfe line search compares against NaN (always false)
and cannot backtrack. Fix = clamp `uvp` before the norm. **60 catastrophic trials → 0.**
Makes `fallback_mm=150` redundant (identical to 3dp on the fixed solver; it *hurts* free-bone).
`scripts/ba_refine.py` is UNMODIFIED — the fix lives only in `cache_ba_variants.py` / scratch.

**Lag alignment uses the wrong estimator.** `gnn_build_dataset.py:222` shifts by the WRIST-only `lag`

applied. 48 trials admitted on the multi metric are aligned by a wrist lag that failed (one by 151 fr).
`scripts/lag_stacked.py` implements the principled version — stack the Fisher-z cross-correlation
CURVES and take one argmax, rather than argmax over 8 per-signal lags. Better peak on 90% / margin on
94% of trials, and yields a usable confidence (margin). Does NOT change PV (window-max), but matters
for Table I and the timing measures.

## Measured, no action

  (post-hoc) is the opposite — best on every column. Don't put smoothing inside the solve.
- **Free-bone (variance, not pinned-length) + SmoothNet** is marginally the best variant
  (r_s 0.875 / R² 0.804 vs shipped 0.859/0.784) but carries a −6% bias. Not shipped.
